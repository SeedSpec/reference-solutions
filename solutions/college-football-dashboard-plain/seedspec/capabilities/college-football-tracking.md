# `org.seedspec.examples.college-football-tracking` 1.0.0

A provider supplies a season-oriented dashboard for a configured watchlist of
college football teams. Consumers may rely on stable team identity, explicit
game states, season-scoped schedules and results, source-identified rankings,
defined comparison metrics, freshness reporting, and accessible alternatives
for data-critical visualizations.

The capability does not require a particular data provider, school conference,
framework, chart library, visual system, hosting environment, or account model.
It does not include wagering, predictions, recruiting, ticketing, video, or
official data-editing authority.
