# `org.seedspec.examples.customer-onboarding-coordination` 1.0.0

A provider turns one authorized customer handoff into one stable onboarding
case with required milestones, explicit ownership, visible blockers,
configuration-governed updates, escalation, and auditable completion.

Consumers may rely on idempotent start behavior, preserved source attribution,
one current milestone state, and evidence that distinguishes a notification
sent from an outcome achieved. The capability does not require a particular
CRM, project tool, messaging provider, automation platform, or runtime.
