# `org.seedspec.examples.operations-workbench` 1.0.0

A provider supplies an internal, role-aware workbench that combines an
operational overview with a searchable record queue and governed record
actions. Consumers may rely on explicit metric definitions, visible data
freshness, stable record identity, reproducible filtering, attributed changes,
and exports that reflect the user's authorized view.

The capability does not require a specific business domain, data source,
framework, visualization library, or deployment environment.
